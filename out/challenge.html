Week 1

Find Your First Signal

Validate your idea. Talk to humans. Find your early adopter.

Complete this formula: 'I help [specific person] achieve [specific outcome] using [your solution].' Example: 'I help solo consultants land 3 more clients per month using my outreach template system.' This isn't abstract — it should pass the 'so what?' test.

Today's Action

Write your one-liner. Then stress-test it: Would a stranger immediately understand who this is for?

Find 5 people who fit your target customer profile. Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter DMs, or email. Your goal is NOT to sell — it's to understand their current alternative. Ask: 'What's the hardest part about [goal they have]? What have you tried?'

Today's Action

Send 5 messages today. Track responses in a spreadsheet. Even 'no thanks' is data.

Look through your responses from Day 2. Find the one person who replied with genuine interest or enthusiasm. This is your early adopter candidate. Reach out directly and ask if they'd take a 15-min call to help you shape the product.

Today's Action

DM your most promising prospect: 'Hey [name], would you be open to a quick chat? I'm building [brief description] and think you might have insights.'

Why are you building this? Who are you building it for? Write 200 words about your personal connection to this problem. This becomes your 'About' page, your Twitter bio, and your anchor when things get hard.

Today's Action

Write your origin story. Share it with your cohort. Ask: 'Does this resonate? Would you buy this?'

Find your 3 closest competitors. Sign up for their free trials, read their documentation, check their G2/Capterra reviews. Document: What do they charge? What do users complain about most? What are they missing?

Today's Action

Create a comparison table: Price | Core Feature | Biggest Complaint | What's Missing. Identify your wedge.

Be specific. Not 'make money' — set an exact MRR target, a customer count, and a churn threshold. Write it down. Example: '$1,000 MRR from 10 customers at $99/mo with <5% monthly churn.'

Today's Action

Write your 30-day target in the format: 'By Day 30, I will have $[X] MRR from [Y] paying customers.'

Compile everything you learned this week. Which conversation revealed the most about what people actually need? What's the gap in the market you've identified? Share this with your cohort before moving on.

Today's Action

Post in your cohort channel: 'The biggest thing I learned this week is [X]. This changes my approach because [Y].'

🚀
Week 2

Ship Something Real

Launch your landing page. Set up payments. Get in front of humans.

Use Carrd.co (free), Framer, or even a well-formatted Notion page. Your page needs: (1) A headline that passes the 'so what?' test, (2) 3 bullet points on what you do and for whom, (3) An email capture form. Ship it today.

Today's Action

Publish your landing page. Send the link to your cohort. Get feedback within 24 hours.

Write a Twitter/X thread, LinkedIn post, or blog that explains the problem you're solving. Not your product — the PROBLEM. Frame it as a story or observation, not a pitch. Include a link to your landing page.

Today's Action

Publish 3 posts today. Track which one gets the most engagement. Double down on that format.

Create a Stripe account if you haven't. Set up a $99/month subscription product. Configure your customer portal, billing portal, and at least one webhook (to Zapier or email). You cannot charge if your payment is broken.

Today's Action

Complete Stripe onboarding. Test your own payment link by purchasing $1 of your own product.

These are not broadcast DMs. These are specific, personalized messages to real people. Reference something they posted, their job, or their company. The opener should be about THEM, not you.

Today's Action

Write 15 unique messages in a spreadsheet. Column 1: Person | Column 2: Personalization hook | Column 3: Message body.

Pick your 5 best-fit prospects from Day 11. Send them TODAY. Track who opens, who replies, who converts. The goal is to start real conversations — not close sales.

Today's Action

Send 5 messages. Log them in your tracker. Set a reminder to follow up in 3 days.

Go through every reply you've received — from social, outreach, and your landing page. Reply to each one personally. If they're interested, offer a call. If they're not, ask what would make it more compelling.

Today's Action

Respond to all messages within 24 hours. Log any objections you hear — they'll inform your pitch.

Check your landing page analytics. How many visitors? How many email signups? What's your conversion rate? A good rate is 5-10% of visitors signing up. If lower, test a new headline or simplify your offer.

Today's Action

Calculate: (Email signups ÷ Page visits) × 100 = Conversion rate. If under 5%, change your headline and test again.

Week 3

Get Your First Dollar

Make the ask. Close the sale. Your first paying customer changes everything.

Take stock. Do you have: (1) A landing page? (2) A payment link? (3) At least one interested prospect? If any are missing, fix them today. Revenue requires all three.

Today's Action

Checklist: [ ] Landing page live [ ] Stripe configured [ ] At least 1 warm prospect. If any are missing, fix immediately.

If you don't have a live product, use Gumroad, LemonSqueezy, or Typeform to collect payments 'early access.' Offer $79/mo for first 10 customers (vs $99 later). Create scarcity: 'Only 10 spots at this price.'

Today's Action

Set up early bird offer. Publish your paid waitlist. Send to everyone who's shown interest.

Write a thread or post announcing what you're building and that you're taking early customers. Frame it as a story of discovery, not a product announcement. Proof-read it. Then publish.

Today's Action

Publish launch content. Include your payment link or landing page. Ask 3 friends to share.

Find 3 Reddit communities, Discords, Slack groups, or Facebook Groups where your target customers discuss related problems. Don't pitch — just observe and contribute genuinely. Note what questions get asked repeatedly.

Today's Action

Join 3 communities. Lurking is fine. Share 1 genuinely helpful comment. Note top 3 questions people ask.

Document what happens after someone pays: (1) Welcome email with login info, (2) Getting started guide, (3) Day 3 check-in, (4) Day 7 feedback request. Write these as templates you can automate.

Today's Action

Write 4-email onboarding sequence in a Google Doc. You'll automate this in Week 4.

Text, DM, or email your #1 warm prospect from this week. Say: 'Would you pay $99/month for [product]? I'm launching next week and looking for early users.' If they say yes, send the payment link immediately.

Today's Action

Contact your hottest prospect. Ask for the sale directly. Send payment link same day.

Review everyone you've talked to this week who hasn't converted. Send a follow-up. People don't convert on first contact. Follow up 2-3 times before giving up.

Today's Action

Send follow-up messages to everyone who went cold. Track responses. If still no response after 3 attempts, move on.

⚙️
Week 4

Build Your Engine

Systematize what worked. Automate your funnel. Set your 60-day target.

If you made your first dollar: congratulations. Document exactly what action produced it. If you didn't: identify the blocker. Was it traffic? Conversion? Something else? Tell your cohort.

Today's Action

Revenue check: What worked? What didn't? What's your next move? Post in cohort channel.

If you've been underpricing, now's the time to test $99 or $149/mo. Add a yearly option (10-20% discount). Your early customers validate the concept — now optimize for revenue per customer.

Today's Action

Update your Stripe product to $99/mo. Add yearly option ($990/year = 2 months free). Announce in your content.

Set up your email sequence in ConvertKit, Mailchimp, or Loops. Configure Stripe webhooks to auto-tag customers. Remove every manual step between payment and delivery.

Today's Action

Set up email automation: Welcome → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14 check-in. Test the full flow yourself.

Create one asset designed to drive traffic: a Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, blog tutorial, or YouTube short. Focus on teaching something related to your product, not selling it.

Today's Action

Publish 1 piece of content. Distribute it in 2+ channels (Twitter + a community you joined). Track referral traffic.

Talk to everyone who's paid. Ask: 'What's the #1 thing this is helping you with? What's missing?' Fix the #1 complaint before scaling.

Today's Action

Send 3 customer interviews. Document feedback. Implement the most critical fix within 48 hours.

Look at your data: Which content format got the most traction? Which outreach method got responses? Which channel drove signups? Put all effort into that one channel for the next 3 days.

Today's Action

Identify your top-performing channel. Commit to publishing there daily for the next week.

Track these weekly: MRR, New Customers, Churn Rate, Conversion Rate (visitors → signups → customers). Create a simple spreadsheet. Update it every Monday morning.

Today's Action

Create your metrics spreadsheet with: MRR | New Customers | Churn | Conversion Rate. Check in weekly.

Write down what you did, what worked, what didn't. This becomes: (1) Your content bank for the next 6 months, (2) Your reference when you forget, (3) Your recruiting story later.

Today's Action

Write a 'What I Did' document: Week-by-week breakdown. Save it somewhere safe.

Based on what you learned, set a new MRR target. Not $1K this time — think bigger. What would $5K MRR enable? $10K? Write the number. Tell your cohort. Then reverse-engineer the week-by-week path to get there.

Today's Action

Set your 60-day MRR goal. Post it in cohort: 'My 60-day target is $[X] MRR. My weekly checkpoint is [Y].'
